# Gameplay feel

The V2 driving pass removes the scrolling-ground illusion. The player vehicle now owns an actual forward world coordinate, authored environment objects remain fixed, and a damped chase camera follows the car. The procedural vehicle was rebuilt with a rounded body, hood, glass cabin, mirrors, wheels, lights, bumper and rear details. The harbor route now contains fixed buildings, containers, trees, lamps, obstacles, police vehicle and a modeled ship ramp.

WASD/arrow input controls acceleration, braking and lane steering. The normal chase distance before the first authored fault was extended so the player can establish vehicle feel before entering investigation.
